## 👨‍💻 About Me

**DevOps Engineer | Kubernetes & Cloud Native | AWS & Azure | Terraform | CI/CD**

DevOps Engineer with nearly four years of experience across **infrastructure administration, automation, cloud platforms, and containerized environments**. I design, deploy, and improve reliable, scalable, and secure infrastructure that helps teams deliver applications faster and operate them with confidence.

My core expertise is **containerization and orchestration** with **Docker, Kubernetes, OpenShift, and Helm**, supported by practical work on **AWS, Azure, Terraform, Terragrunt, Ansible, AWX, and GitLab CI/CD**. I work across Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, observability, cloud platforms, and systems automation to standardize deployments, reduce manual tasks, and strengthen infrastructure resilience.

I am comfortable translating a need into a technical solution, building a **PoC / MVP**, automating its deployment, and producing the documentation needed for operation. Recent work includes **Apache Airflow industrialization on OpenShift and AWS MWAA**, **Keycloak OIDC/SAML integration**, **Terraform/Terragrunt automation**, **OCI image build pipelines**, and **observability with Prometheus and Grafana**.

My approach emphasizes **version-controlled infrastructure, clear documentation, security by design, and collaboration between development, data, infrastructure, support, and security teams**. I am developing my expertise toward **Senior DevOps Engineer** and **Cloud Architect** responsibilities, with a strong interest in **Kubernetes ecosystems, automation, observability, and continuous improvement**.
